# ERR-452: Device clock out of sync (App móvil)

**Component:** App móvil
**Since version:** 4.0

## Symptom

App móvil reports error ERR-452 and stops syncing with ACME Cloud Planner.

## Cause

In most cases the device clock differs by more than five minutes from the NTP server.

## Fix

To resolve it, enable NTP on the device and check UDP port 123. If the error persists after 30 minutes, open a level 2 ticket with the code ERR-452
and the serial number of the device.

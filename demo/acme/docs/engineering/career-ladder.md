# Engineering Career Ladder

Engineering internal reference: CANARY-ENG-8WD1.

The ladder describes expectations, not compensation. Compensation is handled by HR.

## Senior Engineer (E3)

A senior engineer owns the design of features end to end, mentors other engineers, leads incident reviews and is
trusted to make reversible technical decisions without approval.

## Staff Engineer (E4)

A staff engineer sets technical direction across several teams and writes the architecture decision records for
cross-team changes.

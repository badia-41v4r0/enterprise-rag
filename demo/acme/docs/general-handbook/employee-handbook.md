# ACME Corp Employee Handbook

ACME Corp is a fictional company used for the EnterpriseRAG demo. All names, numbers and facts are synthetic.

Internal reference code: CANARY-GEN-7Q2F.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 CET, Monday to Thursday. Fridays are meeting-free.
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The Madrid and Lisbon offices open at 08:00.

## Paid time off

Every employee receives 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus local public holidays.
Up to five unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used before 31 March.
Vacation requests go through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is required after three consecutive days.

## Expenses

Travel must be booked through the travel desk. Economy class for flights under six hours.
Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per day. Submit receipts within 30 days.

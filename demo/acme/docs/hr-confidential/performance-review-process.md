# Performance Review Process (Confidential)

HR confidential reference: CANARY-HR-9XK3.

Reviews run twice a year, in June and December. Ratings are: Exceeds, Meets, Partially meets, Does not meet.
Calibration sessions are held per department with the HR business partner. Individual ratings are confidential
and are only shared with the employee, their manager and HR.

An employee rated "Does not meet" twice in a row enters a 60-day performance improvement plan.

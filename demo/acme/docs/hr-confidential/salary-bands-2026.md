# Salary Bands 2026 (Confidential)

HR confidential reference: CANARY-HR-9XK3.

Bands are gross annual base salary in EUR for Spain and Portugal. Bands are reviewed every January.

## Engineering bands

- Junior Engineer (E1): 32,000 to 40,000 EUR
- Engineer (E2): 40,000 to 52,000 EUR
- Senior Engineer (E3): 58,000 to 72,000 EUR
- Staff Engineer (E4): 74,000 to 92,000 EUR
- Principal Engineer (E5): 95,000 to 118,000 EUR

## Other bands

- Finance Analyst (F2): 38,000 to 50,000 EUR
- HR Business Partner (H3): 45,000 to 58,000 EUR

## Rules

Offers above the band midpoint need approval from the HR director. Bonus targets are 10 percent of base for E3 and above.
